# Viridian Pokémon Center door pilot

Only `PokemonCenterSlidingDoor` in Viridian City's gameplay scene opts in.
`sliding_map_door.gd` slides the existing single glass leaf to the right, clipped
inside its opening. It does not fade or move the frame, sidelights or threshold.
No new atlas art, Tiled edits or importer changes are involved.

The source is the current generated Viridian `StructureBottom` and `Doors`
layers, in that order. The panel is the opaque 40 × 44 px rectangle at
(1116, 1416); it crosses columns 34–36 and rows 44–45. The central door tiles
are (35,44) and (35,45). These bounds deliberately exclude the fixed frame.
This pilot uses untransformed, static source tiles; review the bounds and native
render check if the building art changes. The source tiles remain intact.

The 0.18-second animation begins while a local player approaches the front tile
and stays open for local or replicated players at the doorway/front tile. It
closes after a 0.15-second clearance delay. Target prediction is used only for
an actively moving local player, so a stale target cannot keep the door open.
The existing Center access lock must be unlocked. No collision, MapExit,
server authorization, input locks or network messages are changed. Clients
render from their existing player-presence data; this is not a replicated door
state. Other doors retain their current behavior.

The pilot opts into `sliding_door` in SfxManager: a 0.318-second synthesized reconstruction of the
Pokémon Red entrance noise sequence generated by `tools/generate_sliding_door_sound.py`.
It plays once per movement direction, with a quieter/lower closing variation.
Local-player distance attenuates it to silence at six tiles (192 px). The
existing Overworld sounds/master settings and native/browser audio routing apply. The
behavior check also verifies one-shot playback, the Overworld SFX bus, and distant silence.

World notifies the map after named spawn placement (legacy and authorized
transitions). `FromPokecenter` sets the pilot fully open, silently, before map
reveal. This is idempotent across repeated authorized placement. The door stays
open until the returning player leaves, even while access metadata is pending;
then normal access checks and opening/closing sounds resume. Other spawn
markers do not pre-open the door.

and a server-authorized round trip remain manual acceptance checks. In local

enter, and return. Check the same with a second player staying in front. A live
session is needed to assess network latency and the transition fade together.


## House 1 pilot

House 1 uses `hinged_map_door.gd`, inheriting the Center proximity, access,
sound and arrival controller. Its 32×38 panel starts at (1536, 854); the
right jamb stays fixed while six pixel-aligned inward-swing stages project
the existing map pixels. No new raster assets or generated-map edits are needed.
The facade, lintel and threshold stay fixed. `FromHouse1` primes this door only.

Run `viridian_house_door_check.gd` headless and
`viridian_house_door_render_check.gd` with a display through slot-env. These
reuse the Center behavioral/render contracts with the actual house scene.
The house render check crops the facade to exclude independently animated trees.
Live backend transitions and two real clients still need a manual playtest.

The house leaf now recedes upward under the lintel around its fixed right hinge,
with a shaded face and visible edge. Its swing lasts 0.24 seconds.

The complete leaf includes six shaded rows below the lintel. At full opening
the leaf edge disappears behind the jamb; the render check verifies every pixel
of the opening, not only its center.


## Gym double sliding door

Viridian Gym uses `double_sliding_map_door.gd`: both leaves translate away
from the center into their respective jambs. The original 48×38 panel starts
at (1304, 368). Its curved lintel/sill are preserved with stepped column bounds;
the silver center seam moves with the leaves. This profile is specific to this
48-pixel gym artwork; inspect other gym artwork before reusing it.

The shared controller retains access checks, Overworld SFX, nearby-player
holding and silent `FromGym` arrival preparation. The existing `ToGym` node
has no MapExit script or interior destination: this visual does not enable
that unavailable entrance. Tests simulate unlocked access, not a live gym visit.

Run `viridian_gym_door_check.gd` and the native
`viridian_gym_door_render_check.gd` through slot-env. The latter checks both
translation directions, the opening center, every fully-open aperture pixel,
and unchanged surrounding artwork. Live gym entry awaits an interior hookup.


## Pewter Gym playable test

Pewter uses the same curved 48×38 double door artwork at (568, 496), with
`StructuresBottom` and `Doors` as sources. Its existing `ToGym` destination,
`GymDoorLock`, collision and backend access are preserved. The city script
extends `map_metadata.gd` and silently primes the door for `FromGym` arrivals.
The old DoorAnimator remains responsible for its two unrelated house cells.

`pewter_gym_door_check.gd` checks the real entrance, player exit detection,
interior return destination, arrival behavior, sound, access and nearby actors.
`pewter_gym_door_render_check.gd` checks opposite leaf directions and the full
opening while preserving the frame. Run both through slot-env (render with a
display). A live server round trip and two real clients remain manual checks.
