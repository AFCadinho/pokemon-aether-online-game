# Current 3D move approval

Approved by the user on **2026-10-06**: all **194 currently implemented 3D moves**,
including the red/blue Close Combat revision. The current animation round is complete.
Further changes are individual refinements driven by real gameplay feedback.

The subsequent [15-move elemental batch](elemental-move-batch.md) adds individually authored fire, water, poison, energy and storm effects; these refinements are pending player review.

The subsequent [five elemental refinements](elemental-move-refinements.md) update Dark Pulse, Surf, Blizzard, Earth Power and Overheat; these new presentations are pending player review.

The subsequent [cinematic Z finales](z-move-cinematic-climaxes.md) revise 34 Z-moves; those new finales are pending player review. Supersonic Skystrike is the approved reference for that pass.

The subsequent [707-move extension](extended-move-presentations.md) adds gameplay
catalogue coverage. Those presentations remain **pending player review**; this
approval register still covers only the original 194.

This approval covers the 193-key 2D animation catalog plus Bubble Beam. It does
not claim bespoke effects for every gameplay move or approve future additions.
It is presentation approval, not release certification or deployment authorization.

The 24 dedicated presentations and 170 recipes are listed below. Older review
documents and recipe `review` fields describe their creation/provenance phase;
`presentation_review` and this register record current approval.
