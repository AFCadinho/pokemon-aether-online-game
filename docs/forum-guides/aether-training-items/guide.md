**Aether Band** and **Aether Power Band** are reusable items for wild battles. Give one to the Pokémon you want to benefit: keeping it in your Bag does not activate its effect.

## Quick reference

| Item | Effect for its holder | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aether Band | Restores 1 PP to every move after a wild victory | 1,000 Gems **or** 1,000 card credits |
| Aether Power Band | Receives normal wild-defeat EVs, plus 10 per stat the defeated Pokémon yields, even without participating | 1,000 Gems **or** 1,000 card credits |

Each item is **untradeable**, is **not consumed**, and can be purchased **once per account**. You can buy one of each. Paying with a different currency does not let you buy a second copy of the same item.

## Getting and equipping a Band

Once the update is available:

1. Open the **Gift Store** and choose **Training Items**.
2. Select the Band you want, then choose **Aether Gems** or **Aether Credit Card** payment.
3. Check the 1,000 cost and the untradeable notice before buying. A purchase uses one payment method; it does not combine Gems and credits.
4. In your **Party**, right-click the Pokémon you want to equip and choose **Give held item**. Select the Band from your Bag. You can also manage its item through the Pokémon Summary.

A Pokémon has one item slot, so it cannot carry both Bands at the same time. You can move a Band between your own Pokémon by using **Take held item**, then giving it to another Pokémon. If you release its holder, the Band returns to your Bag.

## Aether Band: restore PP after wild victories

After you **win a wild Pokémon battle**, the holder recovers **1 PP on every move**, including moves it did not use. Each move stops at its own maximum PP.

| PP before the reward | PP after the reward |
| --- | --- |
| 0/35 | 1/35 |
| 39/40 | 40/40 |
| 5/5 | 5/5 |

The holder can sit out the battle and does not have to be your party leader. Equip it before the encounter and keep it in your battle party for the reward.

![01-store-aether-band|1144x704](upload://oLQk9gVPK7eGmTZ7KSc9xoHqepI.png)

*Example Gift Store preview: Aether Band restores PP for the Pokémon carrying it. The preview lists its price and purchase limit.*

It does **not** restore PP after catching a Pokémon, running away, losing, trainer battles or PvP. It does not restore HP or heal status conditions.

## Aether Power Band: train EVs without participation

For each **defeated wild Pokémon**, the holder receives the full normal EV yield, plus **10 EVs for each stat that Pokémon yields**. The holder does not need to enter the battle or be your party leader.

The bonus applies only to the yielded stats:

| Normal EV yield of the defeated Pokémon | Aether Power Band holder receives |
| --- | --- |
| Pidgey: 1 Speed | **11 Speed** |
| 2 EVs in one stat | **12 in that stat** |
| 2 Attack and 1 Special Defense | **12 Attack and 11 Special Defense** |

These examples assume enough EV capacity and no global EV boost. Other stats receive nothing from that defeat. Participating in the battle does not pay the same EV reward twice.

![02-store-aether-power-band|1144x704](upload://nzmnDNCCMf90f4yWK9CRYEwHk4s.png)

*Example Gift Store preview: select Aether Power Band and review Aether Credit Card payment and binding.*

Unlike Exp. Share, this item **does not share EXP**. It adds no item bonus in trainer battles or PvP. Captured Pokémon do not give defeat EVs; escaping or losing does not award this wild-victory reward.

### Stored EVs and allocation

The EV reward goes into the holder's **Stored EVs**. Open **Party → Summary → EVs** to inspect and allocate them to that stat.

Normal limits still apply: allocated and stored EVs together cannot exceed **252 in a stat**, and allocated EVs cannot exceed **510 in total**. An active global EV boost applies to the yield and Band bonus before the capacity limit.

## Co-op wild battles

Each trainer's Bands benefit **their own equipped Pokémon** in the selected co-op team.

- **Aether Band:** restores PP after a wild victory where the encounter settles without a capture. If either wild target is captured, the PP effect does not activate.
- **Aether Power Band:** counts each defeated wild specimen. Two defeated Pidgey yield **22 Speed EVs** for the holder before global boosts and capacity limits. If one is caught and the other defeated, only the defeated Pidgey contributes **11 Speed EVs**.

The EV bonus is per defeated Pokémon, not per move, turn or trainer. Your partner does not receive the Band's holder-only bonus.
