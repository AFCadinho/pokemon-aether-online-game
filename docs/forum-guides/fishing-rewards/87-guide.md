Want to recover a forgotten move or change your Pokémon's moves? Visit the **Move Maniac** or **Move Deleter** in a Pokémon Center.

## Teaching a move with the Move Maniac

1. Speak to the Move Maniac.
2. Choose a Pokémon from your Party.
3. Browse or search its available moves.
4. Check the material needed and how many you have.
5. Choose the move and confirm.

If your Pokémon already knows four moves, choose which one it should forget.

The list only shows moves that this Pokémon can learn. Normal level-up moves are available up to its current level: the Move Maniac does not teach future level-up moves early.

## What does it cost?

The cost depends on where the move comes from. These are the labels you may see in the move list:

| Move group | Cost for one move |
| --- | --- |
| Relearn or Evolution | 1 Heart Scale |
| Egg or Pre-Evolution | 1 Big Mushroom |
| Tutor | 1 Resonite Ore |
| Special or Event | 2 Resonite Ore |
| Legacy or Legacy Event | 3 Tiny Mushrooms |

The chosen material is used when the move is taught. Check the cost shown for that move before confirming.

A move being listed for another Pokémon does not mean yours can learn it too. Availability depends on the species and its move list.

## Collecting Heart Scales (next gameplay update)

<!-- pa-fishing-rewards-2026-10 -->

> **Next gameplay update:** The fishing reward changes below are prepared for the next update and are not live yet. Your current game may still use the previous chances.

Fishing will give Heart Scales through a separate roll after you catch or defeat the Pokémon: **8% with an Old Rod, 10% with a Good Rod and 12% with a Super Rod**. Fishing level does not increase these chances.

This roll is independent of Pearls and other sale treasures, so both rewards can drop from the same fishing battle. You receive one Heart Scale when its roll succeeds. A percentage is not a guarantee after a fixed number of attempts.

See the [Fishing Guide](https://forums.pokeaether.com/t/39) for rod requirements and treasure rewards. Use [Item Dex](https://forums.pokeaether.com/t/95) to compare sources for Heart Scales and other move materials.

## Removing a move with the Move Deleter

The Move Deleter removes moves **for free**.

1. Speak to the Move Deleter.
2. Choose your Party Pokémon.
3. Select the move to remove.
4. Confirm your choice.

Your Pokémon must keep at least one move. You cannot delete its final move.

Deleting a move does not give you materials back. If you want it again, you must teach it again through an available method. Check that it can be relearned before deleting something important.

## What about TMs and HMs?

Use TMs and HMs from your Bag on a compatible Pokémon. TMs are used up when taught; HMs can be used again.

The Move Maniac is useful for the other move groups listed above. You may be unable to change moves on borrowed or rental Pokémon because of their restrictions.
