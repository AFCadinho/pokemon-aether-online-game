Fishing can start wild battles. It also gives Fishing XP and may give treasure after the battle.

> This guide explains fishing in the current alpha. Pokémon and treasure chances may change.

## Learning to fish

Talk to Fishing Guru Sam in House 1 in Vermilion City. Complete his Fishing Lesson to unlock fishing and receive the Old Rod.

Stand next to water and choose a rod from the fishing controls.

## Starting an encounter

1. Stand next to water where fishing is allowed.
2. Face the water.
3. Use your rod.
4. Wait for the bite sign.
5. Press the fishing action again at the right time.

A good hook starts a wild battle.

You can change the fishing key under **Settings → Controls**.

The map, rod and your Fishing level decide which Pokémon can appear.

## Fishing levels and rods

Fishing has levels from 1 to 100. Catching or defeating a Pokémon in a fishing battle gives Fishing XP.

| Rod | Fishing level | Regional Badges | Fishing XP |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Old Rod | 1 | 0 | 10 |
| Good Rod | 10 | 3 | 15 |
| Super Rod | 25 | 6 | 25 |

You must also own the rod.

You need 25 XP for Fishing Level 2. Each next level needs 5 more XP than the level before it.

## Current species progression

Your map still decides which species are there.

- **Old Rod:** Magikarp at Lv. 1, Tentacool at Lv. 5 and Poliwag at Lv. 8.
- **Good Rod:** starts at Lv. 10. Later levels add Poliwag, Goldeen and Horsea.
- **Super Rod:** starts at Lv. 25. Later levels can add Gyarados, Staryu, Slowpoke, Horsea, Psyduck, Seaking, Poliwhirl, Kingler, Dratini, Slowbro, Grimer and Dragonair.

Unlocking a species does not mean it appears on every map.

## Fishing rewards (version 0.3.105)

After you catch or defeat a normal fishing encounter, two separate reward rolls are made:

| Rod | Heart Scale chance | Base sale-treasure chance |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Old Rod | 8% | 20% |
| Good Rod | 10% | 30% |
| Super Rod | 12% | 40% |

The Heart Scale roll gives **one Heart Scale** when it succeeds. Its chance depends on the rod and does not increase with Fishing level.

The sale-treasure roll gives **one item** when it succeeds. Fishing levels above Level 1 add **0.05 percentage points per level**, up to **3 extra percentage points** at Level 61. This bonus applies only to sale treasure.

The rolls are independent. You can get no item, only a Heart Scale, only sale treasure, or both. For example, you can find a Pearl and a Heart Scale after the same battle. Missing the bite, fleeing or losing does not give either fishing roll.

### Which sale treasures can each rod find?

| Rod | Possible sale treasures |
| --- | --- |
| Old Rod | Pearl, Stardust |
| Good Rod | Pearl, Big Pearl, Stardust |
| Super Rod | Big Pearl, Pearl, Stardust, Pearl String |

Heart Scales are **not** part of this sale-treasure table. Each rod uses the relative weights of its remaining sale items, so the overall treasure chance is not the chance for each individual item. The Item Dex shows each item's effective chance per fishing battle won or Pokémon caught.

### Fishing for money

Sell the treasures you collect at a Pokémart. Current NPC sale prices are **1,000 Pokédollars for a Pearl**, **4,000 for a Big Pearl**, **1,500 for Stardust** and **10,000 for a Pearl String**. These are NPC prices, not player-trade prices.

Keep Heart Scales when you need to relearn moves with the Move Maniac. The cost is one Heart Scale for a Relearn or Evolution move; selling one to an NPC gives only 50 Pokédollars.

An active **Global Skill EXP Boost** doubles the Fishing XP from a completed fishing battle. It does not double treasure chances, Heart Scale chances or Pokémon battle XP.

## Other systems

Fishing battles can give normal wild-battle rewards, including the current rarity-based Aetherite reward for eligible wild Pokémon.

They can also count for the Shiny Tracker when the Pokémon matches your hunt.

## Quick tips

- Catch or defeat the Pokémon to get Fishing XP and a treasure chance.
- Raise your Fishing level and earn Badges to use better rods.
- Check the Pokédex Locations tab when you are looking for one species.
