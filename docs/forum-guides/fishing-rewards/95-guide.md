Need a Heart Scale, mushroom or another item? The **Item Dex** helps you look up what an item does and where it can be found.

## Looking up an item

![Open Item Dex using the book icon|360x260](upload://pSxXUY8k3kdP53wvuY2CRkBuPHp.png)

*Open the Item Dex using the book icon in the top-right corner.*

1. Open **Item Dex** in the game.
2. Enter the item's name in the search box.
3. Select the item.
4. Read its description and effect.
5. Look at **WHERE TO GET** for its sources.

![Search for Heart Scale in Item Dex|962x652](upload://yVlyN2T8bzNYHg0dnHw0YdwadMS.png)

*Enter an item name, then select the matching result to see its details.*

You can search for items you do not currently own. Finding an item in the catalog does not mean it is available in every area or at your current story stage.

## Understanding WHERE TO GET

An item may have several sources. Depending on the item, these can include:

- **Shops:** check the vendor, location, price and currency.
- **Wild Pokémon:** check whether the source is a battle drop or a held item.
- **Fishing treasure:** check which rod and activity are required.
- **Thieving:** check the target and requirements.
- **Rock Smash:** check the rocks or areas listed.
- **Quests and NPC rewards:** check the named task or person.
- **World pickups or boxes:** check the listed location or contents.

Read the requirements too. A source may need a quest, skill level, rod or access to a particular place.

## One-time or repeatable?

- **One-time:** you can collect that particular reward once.
- **Repeatable:** the activity can provide more of the item, subject to its own rules and limits.

Repeatable does not mean unlimited at once. For example, daily rocks still follow their daily limits.

## What does a chance mean?

Some sources show a percentage. Read the wording beside it: a chance may apply after a successful theft, a fishing battle won or Pokémon caught, or a completed wild encounter.

A percentage is **not a guarantee**. A 10% chance does not promise one item after exactly ten attempts.

If the chance changes with your skill level, the Item Dex may show a range. Check the requirements and the activity guide before deciding where to collect items.

## Fishing sources (next gameplay update)

<!-- pa-fishing-rewards-2026-10 -->

> **Next gameplay update:** The fishing reward changes below are prepared for the next update and are not live yet. Your current game may still use the previous chances.

Heart Scales will have a separate, fixed chance after a fishing battle won or Pokémon caught: **8% with an Old Rod, 10% with a Good Rod and 12% with a Super Rod**. Fishing level does not increase these Heart Scale chances.

Sale treasures have a different roll: **20% / 30% / 40% base chance** by rod, plus the Fishing level bonus of up to three percentage points. The Item Dex will show each sale item's effective chance after its table weight is applied. That is why a Pearl's chance differs from the total treasure chance.

The two rolls are independent, so a Heart Scale and a sale treasure can drop from the same battle. Fishing source cards will explain this and include both wins and catches. See the [Fishing Guide](https://forums.pokeaether.com/t/39) for the full reward rules.

## Example: materials for the Move Maniac

Before spending time collecting materials:

1. Visit the Move Maniac and check the material needed for your chosen move.
2. Search that material in the Item Dex, such as **Heart Scale, Big Mushroom, Tiny Mushroom or Resonite Ore**.
3. Compare the available sources and their requirements.
4. Choose a source you can currently use.

This helps you avoid collecting the wrong material.

## What if no usable source is listed?

Some entries have no known repeatable source, are not currently obtainable, or refer to a vendor that is not open yet.

That does not mean the item will drop from a random Pokémon. Use the sources shown and check your story progress.

The item's **Base price** is reference information. Check the actual shop listing and payment currency before buying.
