Rock Smash lets you break fixed rocks to earn Skill XP and find useful items. The rocks stay in the same places and return every day.

## How to unlock Rock Smash

1. Complete the lesson at the Trainer School.
2. Travel to Pewter City.
3. Speak with Karate Master Kenji near his four training rocks.
4. Accept his lesson and receive HM Rock Smash.
5. Teach Rock Smash to a compatible Pokémon.
6. Break all four training rocks and return to Kenji.

You do **not** need a Gym Badge to use Rock Smash outside battle.

Completing Kenji's lesson gives you **360 Rock Smash XP** and a **Black Belt**. Together with the XP from the four rocks, this takes the skill to Level 5.

## How to break a rock

Stand directly beside a Rock Smash rock, face it and interact with it.

You must own **HM Rock Smash**. You also need either:

- a Pokémon in your Party that knows Rock Smash; or
- a Rock Smash Charm.

The Charm replaces the Party move, but it does not replace the HM.

Every successful smash gives:

- 35 base Rock Smash XP;
- Pokédollars, with the amount based on the rock requirement and your Rock Smash level;
- a **30% chance** for one regular treasure item;
- a possible complete fossil from Level 20 onward as an extra reward;
- in the upcoming update, an independent Legendary Mining Drop roll based on the rock's tier.

An active **Global Skill EXP Boost** doubles the Rock Smash XP. It does not double items or fossil chances.

## Pokédollar rewards

Every smashed rock always awards Pokédollars. The rock requirement sets the base reward, then your Rock Smash level applies a multiplier. The final amount is rounded to the nearest Pokédollar.

| Rock requirement | Base reward |
|---:|---:|
| Level 1 | 100 Pokédollars |
| Level 5 | 150 Pokédollars |
| Level 10 | 250 Pokédollars |
| Level 20 | 400 Pokédollars |
| Level 50 | 700 Pokédollars |
| Level 75 | 1,000 Pokédollars |

| Your Rock Smash level | Money multiplier |
|---:|---:|
| Levels 1–9 | 1.00× |
| Levels 10–24 | 1.25× |
| Levels 25–49 | 1.50× |
| Levels 50–74 | 1.75× |
| Levels 75–100 | 2.00× |

For example, a Level 50 rock pays 700 Pokédollars at Rock Smash Level 1, or 1,225 Pokédollars at Rock Smash Level 50.


## Items you can find

Regular treasure is a **30% chance per smashed rock**, not a guaranteed reward. When the roll succeeds, possible finds include:

- Pearl, Stardust, Hard Stone and Nugget;
- Big Pearl and Star Piece at higher levels;
- Big Nugget and Comet Shard at later milestones;
- **Resonite Ore** at every reward tier.

Higher levels improve the reward table. Resonite Ore is one of the materials used by the Move Maniac. Items go directly into your Bag.

## Legendary Mining Drops — upcoming gameplay update

**The ores, mining tiers, bad-luck protection and museum buyer below are prepared for the next gameplay update and are not deployed yet.**

Every rock has a mining tier based on its **required Rock Smash level**. Raising your skill does not change a low-tier rock into a higher-tier rock.

A successful smash has an independent chance to award **one Legendary Mining Drop** of that rock's tier. This bonus roll does not require a regular treasure or fossil to drop: all three can appear from the same rock. The ore goes directly into your Bag.

| Rock tier | Rock requirement | Legendary Mining Drop | Base chance per smash | Sale value |
|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| Tier 1 | Level 1 or 5 | Emberite Ore | **1%** | **10,000 Pokédollars** |
| Tier 2 | Level 10 | Voltite Ore | **0.75%** | **25,000 Pokédollars** |
| Tier 3 | Level 20 | Glacite Ore | **0.5%** | **50,000 Pokédollars** |
| Tier 4 | Level 50 | Astralite Ore | **0.35%** | **100,000 Pokédollars** |
| Tier 5 | Level 75 | Aetherium Ore | **0.25%** | **250,000 Pokédollars** |

These are base chances per successful smash, not guaranteed rewards. The normal once-per-UTC-day rock limit still applies. A Global Skill EXP Boost increases XP; it does not multiply ore chances or sale prices.

After the update, **Skills → Rock Smash → Rocks** will show each rock's mining tier. **Rewards → Legendary Mining Drops** will show the ore, required rock level, your current chance and sale value. Item Dex source cards will list locations containing rocks of the matching tier.

### Bad-luck protection for tiers 4 and 5

The game counts successful smashes that do not give the ore **separately for each tier**.

- **Tier 4:** protection begins after **572** tier 4 smashes without Astralite Ore.
- **Tier 5:** protection begins after **800** tier 5 smashes without Aetherium Ore.

From the next smash onward, each additional miss increases the chance by 5% of that tier's base chance, up to five times the base: **1.75% for tier 4** and **1.25% for tier 5**. Finding the ore resets only that tier's counter.

Counters survive relogging and daily rock resets. Smashing lower-tier rocks does not advance higher-tier protection. Failed attempts and repeated requests for an already-smashed rock do not advance the counter.

### Selling all ores to Collector Hugo

**Collector Hugo** buys these five Legendary Mining Drops at the **right blue counter inside the Pewter Museum of Science**.

1. Enter the museum from Pewter City. The entrance lobby is free.
2. Pay **Museum Attendant Iris 100 Pokédollars** to pass the blue barriers into the exhibition side. Admission stays valid for that visit; leaving is free.
3. Stand beside Hugo and interact.
4. Review your ore types, quantities and the complete Pokédollar offer.
5. Choose **Sell all ores** to sell every Legendary Mining Drop in your Bag, or **Cancel** to keep them.

![Upcoming ore sale confirmation|1088x768](upload://8WkhUS5qGwlUPh1cvuSNlXspqtF.png)

*Example of the upcoming confirmation: two Emberite Ore and one of each other ore sell for 445,000 Pokédollars. The offer uses your actual Bag quantities.*

Only the five Legendary Mining Drops are included. **Resonite Ore**, fossils and other items remain in your Bag. Items reserved for a trade or pending loan cannot be sold; the offer must also fit your wallet limit. If your ore stacks change before confirmation, Hugo requires a new offer.

Fossil revival remains in **Cinnabar**. Hugo buys mining ores rather than reviving fossils.

## Fossils

From Rock Smash Level 20 onward, every smashed rock has a small chance to give a complete fossil as an extra reward.

- **Level 20:** Helix Fossil and Dome Fossil become available.
- **Level 50:** Old Amber is added.
- **Level 100:** the fossil chance reaches its current maximum.

The chance starts at **0.25% per rock** at Level 20 and can grow to **0.65% per rock** at Level 100.

You receive the complete fossil immediately. There are no fossil fragments to collect.

## Useful milestones

- **Level 1:** training rocks and basic treasure;
- **Level 5:** more locations and better valuable-item chances;
- **Level 10:** Big Pearl and Star Piece;
- **Level 20:** complete fossils;
- **Level 25:** Big Nugget;
- **Level 50:** Old Amber and Comet Shard;
- **Level 75:** best current treasure table;
- **Level 100:** maximum current level and fossil chance.

Karate Master Kenji can repeat his Rock Smash explanations after you complete the lesson.
