You can change your 3D battle quality under **Settings → Graphics**. Choose a lower setting for smoother performance, or a higher setting for a sharper picture.

| Setting | What to expect |
|---|---|
| **Low** | The lightest option. Battles look softer, with no shadows and less smooth edges. |
| **Medium** | A balance between picture quality and performance. Adds shadows and smoother edges. |
| **High** | A sharper picture, with the same shadows and edge smoothing as Medium. |
| **Ultra** | The sharpest picture available, with extra edge smoothing. The most demanding option. |

On desktop, High already uses full resolution; Ultra mainly makes edges smoother. On Android, the difference in sharpness depends on your screen.

## Which setting should I use?

If battles stutter, try lowering the quality by one step. If everything runs smoothly, try a higher setting and see which you prefer.

You can also choose a separate **frame limit**:

- **30 FPS:** Less smooth, but can help reduce heat and battery use.
- **60 FPS:** Smoother movement.
- **120 FPS or Unlimited:** Desktop only. Allows smoother movement if your computer and screen support it, but can use more power. Your display settings may still limit Unlimited.

Quality changes apply from your **next battle**. Frame limit changes apply immediately.

These settings affect the 3D battle picture. Menus, text and 2D graphics stay sharp, and every setting uses the same Pokémon models and gameplay rules.
