Some Abilities help outside the battle itself. They can influence a wild Pokémon's Nature, improve item drop chances or find an extra item after a wild victory.

This guide covers **Synchronize**, **Compound Eyes**, **Super Luck** and **Pickup** as implemented for PokeAether. Their field rules can differ from other Pokémon games.

## Quick reference

| Ability | Field effect | Where the Pokémon must be |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Synchronize | 50% chance to copy its Nature to a new wild encounter | Party slot 1 |
| Compound Eyes | Increases eligible item drop chances | Party slot 1 |
| Super Luck | The same item drop bonus as Compound Eyes | Party slot 1 |
| Pickup | 10% chance per eligible Pokémon to find an item after a wild victory | Any Party slot, with an empty item slot |

**Fainted Pokémon can activate all four effects.** The Pokémon with the Ability does not have to participate in the battle.

## Choose your party leader

Your **party leader** is the Pokémon in the first slot, at the top of your Party. For Synchronize, Compound Eyes and Super Luck, put the right Pokémon there **before starting the encounter**.

To change your leader, drag a Pokémon into the first slot, or right-click another Party Pokémon and choose **Set as lead**.

![01 party leader|306x181](upload://nxggXJ03R1VlKSqCMEpCdXjwcos.png)

*Example party: Abra is the leader in slot 1. Meowth can use Pickup from another slot.*

Only the leader supplies the Nature or drop bonus. Putting another Compound Eyes or Super Luck Pokémon farther down the Party does not add a second bonus.

## Synchronize: find a matching Nature

With **Synchronize** in slot 1, each new wild encounter has a **50% chance** to copy the leader's Nature. If the effect does not activate, the wild Pokémon uses its normal random Nature, which can still happen to match.

**Example:** put a **Modest Abra with Synchronize** in slot 1. When Synchronize activates, the wild Pokémon will also be Modest. This helps when looking for a Nature that suits a Pokémon's moves and role.

![02 synchronize nature|646x426](upload://rxAiieB4qw0W2dDYyNnifx0QCNf.png)

*Example: check both the Ability and Nature in Abra's Summary. This one has Synchronize and Modest.*

The Nature is decided when the encounter is created. Switching your leader during that battle does not change it. You still need to catch the Pokémon to keep it.

## Compound Eyes and Super Luck: more item drops

**Compound Eyes** and **Super Luck** give the same field bonus when their Pokémon is in slot 1. Choose either Ability; their drop bonuses do not stack.

The bonus covers eligible wild item drops, including:

- Materials such as Mushrooms and Resonite Ore.
- Evolution items.
- **Mega Stones**.
- Fishing treasure and fishing Heart Scales.

Normal species and encounter requirements still apply. A Pokémon must already be eligible to drop the item, and each item's defeat or capture rules remain in place.

### How much does the chance increase?

The increase depends on the original drop chance:

| Original chance | With Compound Eyes or Super Luck |
| --- | --- |
| 0.25% | 1% |
| 1% | 4% |
| 4% | 16% |
| 5% | 20% |
| 10% | About 24.44% |
| 20% | About 33.33% |
| 50% | 60% |

These are examples of how a drop rate changes, not a list of which items every species can drop. Use the **Item Dex** to check an item's sources.

## Pickup: find an extra item after a wild victory

After a **won wild battle**, each eligible Party Pokémon with **Pickup** gets its own **10% roll** to find an item.

The Pickup Pokémon:

- Can be in any Party slot.
- Can be fainted or sit out the battle.
- Must have an **empty item slot**.
- Must still be in your Party when the reward is collected.
- Cannot be a rental or have locked equipment.

The item is placed **on that Pokémon**, rather than directly in your Bag. A successful roll fills its item slot, so remove the item before expecting another Pickup reward from it.

Pickup does not activate after a capture, escape or defeat, or after trainer battles or PvP. A newly caught Pokémon cannot provide Pickup for the encounter in which you caught it.

### What can Pickup find?

The Pokémon's level determines its item pool. Possible rewards include medicines, Poké Balls, Rare Candies, evolution stones, some held items and TMs.

Here are a few examples; each row shows only part of that level range's pool:

| Pickup Pokémon's level | Examples of possible items |
| --- | --- |
| 1–10 | Potion, Antidote, Great Ball |
| 11–20 | Antidote, Super Potion, King's Rock |
| 31–40 | Rare Candy, Dusk Stone, Ether |
| 51–60 | Dawn Stone, White Herb, TM Rest |
| 91–100 | Max Elixir, Leftovers, TM Earthquake |

A successful Pickup roll does not make every item equally likely. Some items are much rarer than others.

### Collect your Pickup item

1. Finish the battle and check your Pickup Pokémon's item slot.
2. Right-click that Pokémon in your Party.
3. Choose **Take held item**.
4. The item moves into your Bag and the Pokémon's item slot becomes empty.

You can also remove the item through its Pokémon Summary or the PC's item controls. **This works for Potions, Poké Balls and TMs too**, even though you cannot normally give those items as battle equipment.

![03 pickup take item|646x426](upload://mKRShdALRb3rX4N9TMllgJsZGCx.png)

*Example: Meowth has Pickup and carries an Antidote. Choose Take held item to move it into your Bag.*

The Pokémon can enter another battle while carrying a found item. It simply cannot find another Pickup item until its item slot is empty.

## Co-op encounters

In co-op, each trainer uses their **own Party** for these effects. Synchronize and the drop bonus apply to that trainer's own wild target; Pickup rewards go to that trainer's own eligible Pokémon.

Pickup still requires a wild victory. A co-op encounter that settles with a capture does not trigger Pickup.
