# Battle nature details

Selected unmodified GLB models from Kenney Nature Kit (2.1, as identified by the included license), CC0.
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
Downloaded 2026-09-26. The upstream license is included in LICENSE.txt.

Only logs, a stump, mushrooms and a lily are bundled; the established forest
art pack supplies the trees, rocks and grass. Models are scaled at placement.
