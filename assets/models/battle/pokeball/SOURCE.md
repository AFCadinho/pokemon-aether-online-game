# Openable Poké Ball

Author: MCStevebrine12.
Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/openable-pokeball-684333ae790942e5b840b3ea42edb8f9

Downloaded by the project owner on 2026-10-05; supplied as openable-pokeball.zip.
Original file: source/random opening pokeball.fbx (renamed only).

The game normalizes the model scale, samples the supplied opening animation,
and composes its own summon, recall and capture choreography and particles.
Ball shell colors may be adapted to the ball used. No animation from AnonimArt
is included. Pokémon names and designs belong to their respective owners.
