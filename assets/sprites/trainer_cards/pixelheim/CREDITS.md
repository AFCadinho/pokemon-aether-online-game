# Jessie and James dialogue portraits

Artwork: **@Pixelheim**, as credited on the sprite sheet supplied by the user
on 2026-10-18. No original source URL was supplied.

`jessie.png` and `james.png` are the two large, separate sprites from the lower
row of that sheet. Only cropping, green-background removal and transparent
padding were applied with local pixel tools. Every opaque source pixel keeps
its original RGBA value; no redraw, palette conversion or rescaling was used.
Both portraits use a 128×128 transparent canvas and the same bottom baseline.

`extraction.json` records the source hash, source rectangles, exact background
mask, placement and output hashes for reproducibility.
