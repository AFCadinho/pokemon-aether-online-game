# Original cave arena study

A standalone, Kanto/Mt. Moon-inspired visual study, not a reconstruction of an
official map. All environment meshes and stone materials are generated by our
script. No purchased forest/cave assets or new bitmap downloads are needed.
The existing local Dragonite/Roaring Moon prepared catalog is still required;
this does not change rights or distribution of those models.

## Open

```sh
python tools/sprite_factory/prepare_cave_review.py ../.worktrees/slot-c/.tmp/cave-review-01
bash tools/sprite_factory/open_cave_review.sh
```

The preparation destination must be outside the frontend checkout and empty or
marked as our review project. Only six explicit code/shader files are packaged;
no caches, userdata, source assets or machine settings are copied.
Launcher uses slot-c's existing catalog and slot-env isolation. No normal client
settings, battle flow, forest project or production arena is replaced.

Right-drag orbits, wheel zooms, Tab hides controls, Play both tests selected actions.
Camera distance/height is constrained to stay within the chamber walls/ceiling.

## Design

Warm grey-brown stratified rock walls surround a flat sand/stone floor. Two tunnel
mouths recede into darker rock arches; no crystals, lava or fantasy lighting.
Rubble and small stalagmites stay outside the fighting area. Meshes are deterministic
and instantiated identically for the main and material-response render passes.
Walls/roof deliberately do not cast shadows across the entire chamber: Pokémon
use the fixed neutral three-light rig, approved endpoint materials and corrected
High shadow filtering. This is art-directed readability, not physically accurate
cave illumination. The surrounding background and rock palette provide the mood.

Cave review inherits the existing review grounding/action controls; the terrain
height hook returns the flat cave surface instead of calling Terrain3D. The paid
terrain scene is never loaded. Idle geometry is calibrated and independently
remeasured after rendering; other action contact semantics are not yet certified.

## Verification / limits

Preparation tests reject unrelated destinations, allow refresh and exclude forest
assets. Godot smoke rechecks both light rigs, full idle ground clearance, four orbit
captures, seven action starts and viewport cleanup. Local capture directories live
under slot-c/.tmp. This is a first visual candidate, not final production art.
Procedural assembly/calibration is review startup work; cache/bake before shipping.
No full development certification, backend changes, new species or battle UI work.

Local evidence (2026-09-20): `cave-run-04.log` and `cave-capture-04/` under
slot-c/.tmp. Smoke passed with 0.0250/0.0255 ground clearance and four reviewed
orbit captures. On the RTX 3070 Laptop GPU, sampled p95 frame time was 17.357 ms;
this short review run is not a production performance certification.
Run preparation unit tests with `python tools/sprite_factory/test_prepare_cave_review.py`.
